# Factory Owner authority

Tim Brydges is the sole human owner and ultimate authority of Tim's Software Factory.

This authority is unconditional within the Factory:

- Tim may inspect, change, approve, reject, dispatch, pause, stop, resume, or override any work at any stage.
- Tim may approve his own changes and does not require approval from any human, agent, reviewer, controller, workflow, or gate.
- Tim has an always-on personal bypass for repository rulesets and may bypass production-environment protection rules.
- Tim's direct production release and emergency rollback paths impose no approval or self-approval step; his authenticated dispatch is authoritative.
- Every agent, reviewer, controller, workflow, and automated policy is subordinate to Tim's explicit instruction.
- An owner stop or pause order takes effect immediately, revokes active leases, and blocks further automated dispatch.
- Tim's owner actions are recorded in the audit log, but audit recording is not an approval requirement and cannot veto the action.
- Ultimate owner authority cannot be delegated by an agent or inferred from a service credential.

Agent safeguards remain mandatory for agents. Authors may not approve their own agent-produced work, reviewers must remain independent, evidence must remain provenance-bound, and automated transitions default to deny. These controls protect Tim's Factory; they do not govern Tim.
